# 27-card tensor structure

A tensorial and group-theoretic analysis of the 27-card trick.

The project studies the combinatorial structure underlying repeated cyclic
dealing and pile collection. Card positions are represented as digital
addresses in base \(3\), leading naturally to tensor products, Kronecker
products, permutation matrices and group actions.

The main topics are:

- tensor representation of card positions;
- cyclic permutation of tensor factors;
- Kronecker factorization of the complete transformation;
- the action of \(S_3^3\);
- conjugacy classes and permutation invariants;
- digital fibers and reconstruction of hidden positions;
- extension from perfect decks \(N=b^m\) to rectangular decks \(N=ij\);
- the distinction between physical deck permutations and informational
  contraction in the classical 21-card trick.
